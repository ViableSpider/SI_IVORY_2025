Interactions Among Wildtype and Heat-Evolved Photosymbionts Shape Performance of Coral Recruits

Coral Reefs

Elizabeth A. Ivory 1,2, Jos C. Mieog 2, Matthew R. Nitschke 1,3, Madeleine J. H. van Oppen 1,4, David Abrego 2
1Australian Institute of Marine Science, Townsville, QLD, Australia
2Faculty of Science and Engineering, Southern Cross University, Lismore, NSW, Australia
3School of Biological Sciences, Victoria University of Wellington, Wellington, New Zealand
4School of BioSciences, University of Melbourne, Parkville, VIC, Australia

Corresponding Author: Elizabeth A. Ivory (eanneivory@gmail.com)

This study reveals that in-hospite interactions between phenotypically distinct coral symbionts affects the growth and survival of their hosts.
